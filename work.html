Interactive · ml5.js
Dear Dream Self
Face Modification Web App · p5.js + ml5 FaceMesh · 2025
A live webcam experience built with p5.js and ml5's FaceMesh model, tracking the viewer's face in real time and letting them distort, reshape, and reimagine their own features through a set of on-screen tools.
Underneath the playful interaction is a more critical question: what happens when the tools to "fix" your face are this immediate, this accessible? The project sits at the intersection of computer vision and cultural critique — using the very technology that normalizes digital editing to make its effects visible and hands-on.

02
Interactive · Web Story
When the Tooth Dropped
Scroll-Based Storytelling · 2025
A reimagining of the tooth fairy myth as a scroll-triggered web experience, coded from scratch in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Each scroll position unlocks a new beat of the story and small interactive moments the reader has to trigger themselves.
The goal was to recreate the specific wonder and strangeness of a childhood myth.
03
E-Book · AI · Taiwanese Cuisine
呷飽未 Have You Eaten?
AI-Assisted E-Book · 5 Dishes · Pika Animation · 2026
An e-book reimagining five iconic Taiwanese dishes through the lens of artificial intelligence. Each dish started as a hand-drawn sketch, which was then fed into Pika to generate a short animation.
The project doesn't resolve the tension so much as sit inside it: every page is a small argument about authenticity, inevitability, and what's lost or gained when AI gets a hand in food culture and creative practice.

04
Interactive · Web Design
Entropy
Click-Driven Interactive Piece · 2025
An interactive web piece built entirely around clicking as the driving gesture — recreating the scene of entropy through my own visual definition, where each click pushes the composition further from order.
Coded from the ground up, the piece treats disorder as something the viewer causes rather than just observes, turning a physics concept into a hands-on, cumulative experience.

3D · Parametric · Organic
Synthetic Body
Rhinoceros 3D · Grasshopper · Blender
A series of parametric explorations of the human form, built in Rhinoceros 3D and scripted through Grasshopper: branching, vein-like structures that grow from palm to fingertip, alongside 3D print support systems designed to hold that organic geometry together.
Rendered and finished in Blender, the pieces sit at the intersection of body, machine, and nature — reimagining prosthetics not as clinical replacements but as a kind of living architecture, grown rather than assembled.

3D · Cosplay · Costume & Prop Fabrication
Bomb Devil (Reze)
Chainsaw Man · Rhinoceros 3D · 3D Printing · Costume Build
A full cosplay build of Reze's Bomb Devil form, modeled in Rhinoceros 3D and brought into physical form through 3D printing — the same parametric-to-physical pipeline used across my spatial design work, applied here to costume and prop fabrication.
From digital sculpt to finished armor and props, the piece carries over the same attention to structure, fit, and form-making that drives the rest of my 3D practice.

Performance Art · Exhibition
Actions and Gestures
Group Exhibition · Shanghai, China · 2025
A performance piece presented as part of the Actions and Gestures exhibition in Shanghai, China. The work involves applying paper cutouts of my own eyes — rendered in three different emotional expressions — directly onto my body.
Through the repeated action of applying and removing each pair of eyes, the piece explores shifting emotional states, self-surveillance, and the layering (and stripping away) of identity in front of an audience.
